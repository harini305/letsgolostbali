Men’s Circles In Bali & Online

Here at Let’s Get Lost Bali, we are committed to developing and expanding a space for men to connect and grow with other men. Check out the circles already running both in person and online in partnership with Solis Brotherhood.

We run minimum commitment circles, to create a strong container for men to show up.
