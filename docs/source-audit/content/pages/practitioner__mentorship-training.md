🔹 The Mentorship Framework

Every pathway follows this core structure—adapted to your level and goals:

Assessment & Intention
We start with where you are: your experience, your goals, your edge. We set clear intentions and success metrics.

Technique Mastery
Break down breathwork modalities at a granular level: physiology, pacing, contraindications, cues, and adjustments. You'll practice, get feedback, refine, repeat.

Facilitation & Presence
Learn to hold space with confidence. We work on your voice, your timing, your ability to read a room (or a Zoom), and your own regulation as a guide.

Integration & Accountability
Monthly check-ins on your personal practice, teaching goals, and embodiment. We track progress, adjust course, and celebrate wins.

Application
Whether you're practicing for yourself or preparing to guide others, you'll apply your learning in real scenarios—with support.

Accountability & Weekly Goal Setting

A key component of our program is fostering accountability and goal-setting to ensure consistent progress. Each week, students will set clear, actionable goals tailored to their learning and practice journey. Through structured check-ins and mentorship support, participants will stay on track, overcome challenges, and build confidence in their breathwork facilitation skills. This accountability framework ensures that students remain engaged, motivated, and continuously improving, leading to a well-rounded and effective breathwork practice.

Why Accountability & Goal Setting Matters

Accountability and structured goal setting are essential for personal and professional growth. By regularly assessing progress and receiving constructive feedback, students develop discipline, self-awareness, and resilience. This process not only enhances their learning experience but also instills a sense of responsibility and commitment to their breathwork practice. Through goal setting, students can track their development, celebrate milestones, and refine their approach to ensure steady improvement.

Benefits for Students:

✔ Enhanced Focus & Commitment – Clear goals help students stay dedicated to their practice.

✔ Continuous Growth – Regular self-evaluation and feedback foster skill enhancement.

✔ Confidence Building – Tracking achievements empowers students to facilitate sessions with greater assurance.

✔ Stronger Community Support – Engaging with mentors and peers provides motivation and encouragement.

✔ Real-World Readiness – Developing accountability ensures students are prepared to guide clients effectively in their breathwork journey.

By integrating accountability and weekly goal setting, our program ensures that every participant stays motivated, engaged, and fully equipped to become a skilled breathwork practitioner.
