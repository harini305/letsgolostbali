What is Breathwork?

Breathwork is a practice that involves conscious control and manipulation of the breath to achieve specific outcomes. It encompasses a variety of techniques and approaches, but the common thread is the intentional use of breathing to bring about positive changes in the body and mind. From ancient yogic traditions to contemporary therapeutic modalities, breathwork has been a central component of wellness practices across cultures.
