Who is this course for?

This course is for students looking to dive deeper into the aspects of breathwork and themselves, understand the art of teaching, and for those wanting to guide a breathwork session in both 1 on 1 and a group format. Whether you are a complete beginner looking to explore the potential of breathwork for yourself or looking to become an instructor and build your own breathwork practice, this training is for you. You will learn multiple different styles of breathwork along with other techniques that combine great with Breathwork so you have a vast knowledge base to use once you have completed your training.

Want help to manage stress & anxiety for yourself or others? Are you going through a transition period in your life and looking for a practice to assist you? Meet Your Experts

Anthony Ball

Lead Teacher

On my journey of self-discovery, I found Breathwork and meditation as the practice that helped me find balance and grounding. I specialize in embodiment, coming out of the thinking mind, and connecting with intuition and the emotional body.

I offer the space to reconnect to the body and soul by using embodiment practices including shamanic, breathwork, and Osho. I have spent many years in the world of health, wellness, and coaching, creating successful travel, adventure, and hotel businesses in the UK & Bali.

Using breathwork and other powerful techniques I have seen huge shifts within my students. I am dedicated to guiding others to discover peace within and have the confidence, clarity, and motivation to achieve their calling in life.

Alicia Casillas

Pranayama Teacher

I support conscious humans in uncovering their inner voice, giving them the tools to align their lives, and lighting up the way to their most authentic selves.

My life didn’t always look like this. In fact, it used to look a lot different.

After a serious physical injury from years of strenuous exercise, I began searching for a more gentle way to keep active that had a lower impact on my body. My sports psychologist had previously suggested trying meditation to help deal with the pressure that came hand in hand with competitive swimming, and I figured, why not give it a go?

Shortly after being introduced to meditation, I discovered yoga and it didn’t take long until I was hooked. Once I began to experience the dramatic impact these practices had on my body, my mind, and my overall well-being, well, there was no turning back.

After graduating from university, I booked myself a one-way ticket to Asia and embarked on an adventure of a lifetime. I dove headfirst into a new way of life (I was a swimmer at heart after all), and immersed myself fully into yoga and spirituality, learning as much as I possibly could through retreats, workshops, studies, and of course a daily devotional practice. Soon, I discovered that nurturing my mind, my heart, and my body were all key elements to embodying a soul-filled life.

Mental Health Expert

Dr. Brendan is a psychiatrist and health entrepreneur with a passion for holistic mental health and personal optimization. With a background in traditional psychiatry, Dr. Brendan has expanded into non-conventional approaches to mental wellness, integrating cutting-edge mental health strategies with mindfulness, breathwork, and holistic practices. In this talk, Dr. Brendan will explore the intersection of mental health and breathwork, offering insights on how to unlock personal empowerment, practice safely and understand how to work with people with mental health conditions.

Andrea Abian

Anatomy Teacher

Andrea is a Physiotherapist and Yoga Therapist, also a dedicated ashtanga yoga practitioner. With over 15 years of experience as a physiotherapist, she is an expert in pain treatment, structural realignment, and case resolution.

She is also specialized in therapeutic massage, fascia release, and dry needling while doing customized pain treatments, combining various techniques such as Foot Reflexology and neuromuscular bandage (Kinesiotaping).

Andrea studies Yoga Therapy with the Master Sharat Arora, A.G Mohan (Krishnamacharya disciple),  and Viniyoga with Tirumalai Krishnamacharya. Andrea combines her passion for Physiotherapy, Yoga Therapy, and Yin with her vocation for teaching and sharing the sacred tradition of Yoga.
