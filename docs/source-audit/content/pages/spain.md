🌿 Trainings and Retreats in Spain – Coming 2027

In 2027, the spirit of Let’s Get Lost will take root in the wild, sun-drenched landscapes of southern Spain.

Just as Bali offered us a sanctuary to return to the body, Spain will become our European home for deep embodiment, men’s work, breathwork, and Reiki, held in harmony with nature, community, and local wisdom.

Nestled near Cartagena, in a region rich with ancient olive groves, Mediterranean coastline, and quiet mountain valleys, our Spain retreat space will embody the same principles that guide us in Bali:

Sustainability first – solar-powered, water-wise, and built with respect for the land

Local collaboration – working alongside Spanish healers, artisans, and stewards of the land

Embodied presence – offering the same depth of somatic training, men’s circles, and healing retreats you know from Bali

This isn’t an expansion. It’s an evolution, a chance to bring our work closer to Europe, to Anthony’s wife’s homeland, and to a new community ready to come home to their bodies.

Be the first to know.

Retreats and trainings will open for booking in late 2026.

“We’re not leaving Bali, we’re growing roots in two worlds.”
