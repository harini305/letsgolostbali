Meet your lead instructor
Anthony Ball

Anthony didn't find breathwork in a training room. He found it in the wreckage of his own unraveling, and what he discovered changed everything.

After years in health, wellness, and building businesses across the UK and Bali, he recognized that real transformation doesn't happen in the mind. It happens in the body. That insight became the foundation of everything he teaches.

Today, Anthony is one of Southeast Asia's most sought-after breathwork facilitators, specializing in embodiment, trauma-informed breathwork, and the kind of deep nervous system work that creates lasting change. His approach draws from Rebirthing, shamanic practice, Osho-inspired techniques, and over a decade of holding space for individuals and groups in profound states of healing.

He is also a leading voice in men's work, facilitating men's circles and immersions that guide men into emotional depth, authentic power, and a way of being that goes far beyond what society taught them was possible.

What makes Anthony different as a teacher isn't just what he knows, it's that he has lived it. His grounded, no-nonsense background in business and adventure gives him a practical edge that purely spiritual teachers often lack. He doesn't just hold space, he gives you tools, frameworks, and the real-world skills to go out and use them.

Over 50 hours of his most powerful live teaching is inside this training. This is your chance to learn directly from him, wherever you are in the world.
